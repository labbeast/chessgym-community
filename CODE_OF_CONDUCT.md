# Community conduct

ChessGym is for players with different backgrounds, ratings, languages and levels of programming experience. Help make participation useful and welcoming.

- Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking the person.
- Give feedback with concrete examples and room for disagreement.
- Respect people's time, privacy and ownership of their work.
- Welcome questions and corrections. Do not use chess rating or technical experience to dismiss someone.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized content, spam and disclosure of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close a discussion, or restrict participation when necessary. They should explain moderation decisions when it is reasonable and safe to do so, and apply the same expectations to themselves.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse on the platform. To request a private discussion with a maintainer, open an issue asking for a private contact without posting sensitive details or identifying another person publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
